SERVICES

INTEGRATED SPATIAL DESIGN SOLUTIONS

Studio Equilibrium provides a wide array of professional solutions, covering architectural design, master planning, and interior design. We translate complex site coordinates and design briefs into contextual, highly functional spatial realities.

01 // OUTLINE

ARCHITECTURE

We offer comprehensive architectural design solutions spanning conceptual development, construction document preparation (GFC sets), and strict construction administration.

By conducting meticulous site feasibility reviews, project programming, and technical co-ordination, we ensure structure, materiality, and light meet the highest physical and aesthetic standards.

02 // MACRO

MASTER PLANNING

We specialize in macro-scale master planning, whether laying out institutional campus frameworks or designing mixed-use communities.

Our design analysis maps complex site contours, terrain parameters, and solar exposures to position structural masses strategically, optimizing green spaces and circulation.

03 // DETAILED

INTERIORS

We provide complete interior design solutions tailored for residential and small-scale commercial ventures.

Our interior design specialists work in close collaboration with clients to select complementary finishes, custom lighting schemes, furnishings, and decor components, extending external structural logic inside.

*Structural design, MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, & Plumbing), IoT integration, fire suppression systems, and landscaping are coordinated in-house and outsourced to trusted consultants to ensure specialized, comprehensive project success.

OUR PROCESS

ALIGNED TO WORLDWIDE ARCHITECTURAL STANDARDS (RIBA)

01

ONBOARDING & CLIENT VISIONING

The project begins with strategic client consultations to map out programmatic goals, expectations, and initial aesthetics. We collect site data, establish a questionnaire checklist, and begin defining the structural boundaries.

  • Visioning with Client
  • Data Collection
  • Client Checklist
02

PRE-DESIGN FEASIBILITY

We draft a formal project brief and responsibility matrices while establishing schedules. Our team performs site due diligence, reviewing local municipal laws, building codes, and structural constraints to verify viability.

  • Project Brief
  • Responsibility Matrix
  • Schedules
  • Statutory Review
  • Site Feasibility
03

SCHEMATIC DESIGN & CONCEPTS

We translate the brief into initial space planning layouts, zoning proposals, stacking designs, and facade conceptual renders. This phase results in preliminary estimates and basic schematic submittals for sanction approvals.

  • Space Program
  • Facade & Concept
  • Stacking Options
  • Prelim Estimate
  • Sanction Drawings
04

DESIGN DEVELOPMENT

We refine schematic concepts into coordinated design layouts. Draftsmen generate coordinated drawings (Set 01), tender drawings, and bills of quantities (BOQ) to prepare detailed specifications for contractor bids.

  • Coordinated Layouts
  • Tender Drawings
  • Estimates (BOQ)
  • Working Drawings Set 01
05

PROCUREMENT & CONTRACTING

Our team releases GFC (Good For Construction) drawings (Set 02) and establishes the General Contractor program. We assist in evaluating bids and finalizing contracts to prepare for site excavation.

  • Working Drawings Set 02
  • GC Program
  • Contract Finalisation
06

CONSTRUCTION ADMINISTRATION

During the build phase, our studio issues detailed construction revisions (Set 03) and coordinates directly on-site. We conduct regular progress inspections, coordinate engineering installations, and oversee commissioning.

  • Working Drawings Set 03
  • Site Coordination
  • MEP Inspections
  • Commissioning
07

CLOSEOUT & HANDOVER

We conduct final quality walkthroughs to compile snag lists, complete municipal certifications, and handover all final drawings, manual coordinates, and operations manuals back to the client.

  • Final Walkthrough
  • Handover
  • Project Closeout
